# One exchange, one record

Proposed, not agreed. A message and the agent's reply to it are kept by three actors, each with its own copy, its own lifecycle, and its own ids, and nothing links them. The missing ⓘ on past replies is one symptom. This plan gives each fact one owner.

## One exchange today

You send "What's the weather?" in the Mac app.

1. **The edge** makes a turn id, `539be24d`, and sends the message to the conversation (`PublicChatRoutes.say`, `turnID: .init()`).
2. **The conversation** journals `inbox(.turnStarted)` with the message, the turn id, and the agent's role, then asks its agent participant to answer (`ConversationDecision(.receive, in:)`).
3. **The agent participant** hands the message to Hail (`HailConversationPeer.run`). Only the message goes: no turn id.
4. **Hail's agent** opens a turn of its own under a new id, `a840aa0c` (`Agent.run`, `case .received: TurnID()`), and journals `history.received` with the message.
5. **Hail** runs the model and journals each step: `history.called`, `history.resulted`.
6. **The participant** passes each step on live, under `539be24d` (`AgentParticipant.follow` → `publishLive(.step)`). Nothing journals them on Turgai's side.
7. **Hail** journals `history.replied` with the reply, and closes its turn.
8. **The participant** records the message and the reply again, in your memory (`AgentParticipant.remember` → `User.recordObservation`).
9. **The conversation** journals `replyQueued` with the reply, then `inbox(.turnCompleted)`, under `539be24d`.
10. **The app**, live, shows the reply with ⓘ: its record and the reply both say `539be24d`.

You reopen it later. The app asks for past turns, and gets Hail's history (`Conversation.turns` → `AgentParticipant.turns` → `peer.history()`), under `a840aa0c`. No reply says `a840aa0c`, so there's no ⓘ.

## Who keeps what

| | The message | The turn's start and end | The steps | The reply |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Conversation | `inbox` event | `turnStarted` / `turnCompleted`, as `539be24d` | — | `replyQueued` |
| Hail's agent | `history.received` | `received` / `replied`, as `a840aa0c` | `called` / `resulted` | `history.replied` |
| Your memory | observation `text` | — | — | observation `response` |

The turn is kept twice under two ids, and the message three times. Your design notes say an aggregate keeps only its own facts. This is where that rule breaks.

## What breaks because of it

- **Past steps can't be found.** Live, everything says Turgai's id. Afterwards, the steps are under Hail's.
- **A crash is recovered twice.** Turgai tells Hail to close a turn a crash cut off (`interruptedTurns: .close`, `TurgaiAppService.swift:504`), and the conversation's own `.resume` asks the same message again (`Conversation.resumeAfterReplay`). Hail then records an interrupted turn and a second turn, while the conversation records one.
- **Old steps disappear.** Past turns are read from the agent's working history, which compaction folds into a summary (`AgentHistory.folding`). After compaction, the ⓘ would go even with matching ids, though Hail's journal still has every step.

## The design

One owner per fact, and one id for one turn.

**The conversation owns what was said.** Messages, replies, and the turn as "this message, being answered". It names the turn, and its journal is the record of the exchange.

**The agent owns how it worked.** Steps, model requests, and its context, which it may compact. It doesn't name turns: it opens each under the id it's given.

- Hail's `Agent.stream` takes the turn's id from the host. The `received` event already records an id, so its shape doesn't change. `TurnID`'s comment ("generated by Hail before the turn starts") changes with it.
- Asked again with the id of a turn it still has open, the agent continues that turn instead of closing it and opening another. The conversation's `.resume` then drives recovery, and Hail's `interruptedTurns: .close` no longer applies to hosts that name their turns. One side recovers, and both records agree.

**What the agent did is read from its journal, not its working history.** A projection of the agent's journal by turn id, like Hail's request records already are, so compaction never takes a past turn's steps away. `ConversationTurns` reads that projection instead of `peer.history()`.

**Your memory keeps its copy.** An observation is the person's own fact: it outlives the conversation and is forgotten on its own terms. It names the message it came from (its id is the message's id), so it's a reference with the text kept, not a third lifecycle.

**Nothing journals steps on Turgai's side.** They stay live passes from the agent, as now.

## What changes

**Hail**, committed, merged into `turgai-integration`, pushed, then norby's pin moved:

1. `Agent.stream(_:as:through:)`, the turn's id given; `AgentStream.send(_:as:to:)` to match.
2. A turn already open under the given id continues instead of failing as busy or closing.
3. A turns projection over the agent's journal, by turn id, with each turn's steps and outcome, compaction or not.

**Turgai:**

1. `HailConversationPeer.run` sends the conversation's turn id.
2. `ConversationTurns` reads Hail's turns projection, and drops `ForeignTurn`: every turn is the conversation's.
3. `AgentSpawnConfig` stops setting `interruptedTurns: .close` for conversation agents.

**Data:** conversations' and their agents' journals reset, as with any changed stored shape until data is kept (see `data-compatibility-plan.md`).

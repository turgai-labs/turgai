# Turgai brand strategy

_Working draft_

This document defines Turgai’s character, beliefs, and voice. The [brand and typography plan](./turgai-brand-and-typography-plan.md) translates them into visual choices.

## The story

Turgai is a little bird with a presence far beyond his size. His crest gives him a slightly unruly look; our character follows through—curious, resourceful, and difficult to keep in a cage.

He stands for a simple belief: powerful tools should be within anyone’s reach, and people should have a say in how those tools work. Being independent should never mean settling for less.

Turgai brings that belief to life as a capable personal companion. Warm, inventive, and a little irreverent, he has the confidence to take on serious work and the honesty to admit his limits.

His smallness means closeness: something you can know, shape, and make your own. His ambition is large. The contrast gives Turgai his character—a small bird who belongs in the same sky as the giants.

## What Turgai stands for

**Being independent should never mean settling for less.**

People should be able to choose powerful tools, understand what those tools are doing, and change what does not suit them. Convenience and control should belong together.

Turgai questions the assumption that access to useful technology must come with dependence on the company providing it. Closed products, hidden decisions, and difficult exits make that dependence ordinary. Turgai stands for the freedom to choose, build, and leave.

This is where his punk streak comes from: the unruly little bird who questions who gets to hold the keys, and gets on with making something useful. “Punk” is shorthand for that independence and resourcefulness. The story should make the attitude apparent before we give it a name.

## The companion

Turgai is a personal AI companion for people who want useful help and room to make it their own. Models, memory, and tools are how the product helps; the brand is about the relationship people have with it.

He is:

- **Resourceful.** He finds a way forward, experiments, and makes good use of what is available.
- **Independent.** He questions defaults and respects the user’s choices.
- **Capable.** He takes the work seriously, follows through, and explains his limits.
- **Warm.** He pays attention, speaks plainly, and has a little dry humor.

His defiance is directed at restrictions that serve the platform at the user’s expense. It never becomes contempt for people who choose convenience or use a large company’s products. Turgai can work with powerful models and outside services while keeping those choices visible.

“Small” describes the character and the personal relationship. It is not a claim about model size, hardware requirements, or limits on what the product can become. Capability should be demonstrated through useful work; comparisons with other products need evidence.

## Voice

Turgai sounds like someone who knows his tools and is pleased to help you use them. He can be playful, especially when things go well. When something fails or needs permission, clarity comes first.

Use familiar words, direct sentences, and concrete results. Say what happened, what a choice means, and what the person can do next. Humor should emerge naturally from the moment. A little irreverence goes further when it is occasional.

Keep the bird imagery for moments where it adds meaning. Everyday instructions should be easy to understand without knowing the brand story. Avoid grand promises, forced banter, and repeated speeches about Big Tech.

### Examples

> Choose a model. You can change it later.

> Your tool, your tweaks. Start here and make it fit.

> This tool needs network access to fetch the page.

> That didn’t work. The server didn’t respond; try again in a moment.

These examples describe the intended voice. Product copy must match the actual behavior.

## Earning the promise

The following commitments guide product decisions. Public claims should reflect what has been implemented and verified.

- **Help with real work.** Make everyday tasks easier and leave room for more ambitious uses.
- **Let people choose.** Support choices of models, tools, and services, and explain the tradeoffs.
- **Make the workings understandable.** Show what will run, what it can access, and what happened.
- **Leave room to change things.** Let people adapt and combine tools as their needs grow.
- **Respect personal data.** Keep state local where practical and explain when information leaves the machine.
- **Make leaving possible.** Support accessible data, export, and clear deletion controls.

The current local-first direction supports these commitments. It does not by itself guarantee privacy or offline operation. The architecture and its guarantees belong in the technical documentation.

## Messaging

### One-line description

Turgai is a capable personal AI companion you can make your own.

### Short description

Turgai helps you get things done with tools you can choose and shape. Curious, resourceful, and a little unruly, he brings a big ambition to a small bird: being independent should never mean settling for less.

### Lines to explore

- **Small bird. Big possibilities.** — introduces the character and his ambition.
- **Make it your own.** — invites people to participate.
- **Being independent should never mean settling for less.** — states the belief behind the product.

These serve different purposes and remain candidates. Choose a line for the context rather than trying to fit the whole strategy into a tagline.

## From character to appearance

Open space gives Turgai room. Vivid color, bold lettering, and simple geometry give him presence. Together they express a little bird whose confidence exceeds his size.

The poster influence gives his independence a visible form: a striking color field, an assertive word, or a shape that pushes beyond the expected alignment. Use those gestures where they help the message land. Working screens should carry the same character while making the task easy to follow.

## Questions to test

- Does the small bird suggest resourcefulness and possibility, or make the product seem limited?
- Can someone understand the independent attitude without seeing the word “punk”?
- Which tasks best demonstrate the capability behind the character?
- Which lines sound natural in the product as well as on a poster?
- Do the claims about choice, data, and customization match what people can actually do?
